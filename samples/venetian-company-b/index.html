Two materials

Plaster or cement

Lime · marble dust · pigment

Venetian plaster

Built up in thin coats and burnished back as it cures. The polish comes from the tool, not a topcoat, which is why the depth sits under the surface rather than on it.

  • Walls and ceilings
  • Feature and media walls
  • Staircases

Cement · polymer · fine aggregate

Microcement

A few millimetres thick, straight over what is already there — tile, screed, board — so floors rarely need lifting. One continuous surface, no grout lines, no joints to crack.

  • Floors
  • Wet rooms and bathrooms
  • Swimming pool areas

How a project runs

Quoted, agreed, then started

Step 01

Initial discussion

Send us the space and what you're after. We can visit the area to discuss before anything goes ahead.

Step 02

Finish, colour, samples

Once your finish, colour and other requirements are agreed, samples can be provided as part of your package — up to two included, additional samples £80.

Step 03

Quote agreed

Pricing depends on the size of the area and the finish. It is agreed before we commence, and we can suggest alternatives to suit a budget.

Step 04

Date and deposit

Once a date is mutually agreed, a 50% deposit secures the booking — it lets us buy the materials for your project, and it is non-refundable. The balance is due on completion.
